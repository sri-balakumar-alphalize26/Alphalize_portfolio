Alphalize provides robust business intelligence solutions that enable organizations to extract valuable insights from their data. Our sophisticated analytics tools and dashboards allow businesses to gain a deep understanding of their operations, identify trends, and make data-driven decisions. With our business intelligence solutions, our clients can enhance their competitiveness and seize new growth opportunities.

## What we deliver

Most businesses are not short of data — they are short of a place where it agrees with itself. We consolidate your sources into one reporting layer, so the sales figure in a board pack matches the one in the operations dashboard.

- **Modelled metrics** — margin, utilisation and turnover defined once, used everywhere.
- **Self-serve dashboards** — filters and drilldowns your managers can drive without opening a ticket.
- **Alerting** — thresholds that notify the right person instead of waiting for month-end.

## How we work

We hand over documented data models and refresh schedules, so your reporting keeps working when a source system changes or a new product line appears.
