Empower Your Career with Alphalize. At Alphalize, we believe in nurturing fresh talent and providing them with the skills they need to thrive in the tech industry. Our comprehensive Software and Web Development Training and Internship Program is designed to equip students and freshers with the knowledge and experience required to excel in the competitive world of technology.

## What we deliver

The gap between a degree and a first job is rarely knowledge — it is not having worked inside a real codebase. The program is built to close that gap.

- **Fundamentals with rigour** — version control, code review and debugging treated as core skills, not afterthoughts.
- **Project work** — you build features against the same patterns our client projects use, with a mentor reviewing your work.
- **Industry exposure** — how estimates, requirements and releases actually happen on a delivery team.

## How we work

Students in their final year and recent graduates aiming for software or web development roles. Applications go through the [careers page](/en/careers) — mention the training program in your message.
