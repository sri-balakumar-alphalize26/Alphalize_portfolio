This is the role our 24/7 support promise actually rests on. When a customer's counter stops billing
on a Saturday evening, you are the person who works out whether it is the server, the network or the
printer — and gets it running again.

It is a hands-on job with real ownership: you decide how our environments are built, backed up and
monitored, and you will have the standing to fix root causes rather than restart a service and hope.
Some of the work happens at customer sites, putting in the terminals and kiosks you will later be
supporting.
