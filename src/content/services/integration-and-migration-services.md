Alphalize offers seamless integration and migration services to help organizations transition smoothly to new software systems or integrate existing solutions. Our experienced consultants work closely with clients to ensure a seamless transition, minimizing downtime and disruption to operations.

## What we deliver

The risk in any migration is the window where neither the old nor the new system is trustworthy. We close it with parallel running: the new system processes real transactions alongside the old one until the numbers reconcile, and only then do we cut over.

- **Data audit** — find the duplicates and gaps before they land in the new system.
- **Validated loads** — every migrated record reconciled against source totals.
- **Documented rollback** — a tested way back at each phase, not a hope.

## How we work

Where replacing a system is not warranted, we connect it instead — building the APIs and middleware that let your ERP, accounting package and third-party tools exchange data automatically rather than by re-keying.
