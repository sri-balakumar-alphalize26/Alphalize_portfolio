In today's digital age, mobile app development has become a crucial aspect of any business. Mobile apps are not only convenient for users but also help businesses reach a wider audience and increase customer engagement. However, developing a successful mobile app is no easy feat; it requires expertise in a framework such as React Native, and an understanding of user behavior. Moreover, developers must consider the compatibility of the app with various devices and ensure the safety of sensitive data.

## What we deliver

- **Device coverage** — layouts and performance verified across screen sizes and OS versions your users actually carry, not just the newest handset.
- **Offline behaviour** — field and warehouse users lose signal; the app should queue work rather than lose it.
- **Data security** — encrypted storage and transport, with permissions kept to what the app genuinely needs.
- **Store readiness** — privacy disclosures and review requirements handled before submission, not after a rejection.

## How we work

For clients already running an Alphalize ERP, we extend it to mobile — approvals, stock lookups and job updates from the floor or the road, against the same live data as the desktop system.
