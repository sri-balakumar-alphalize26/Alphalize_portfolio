Most of what we build sits on Odoo, so this role is close to the centre of the work. You will be
extending the modules our customers actually run their business on — the ones behind the counter at
a showroom, in a van on a delivery round, or on the back-office machine that closes the books.

Because our deployments are live, the feedback loop is short: a fix you ship this week is in front
of a real business next week. That means more ownership than a role of this size usually carries,
and a low tolerance for changes nobody can explain later.
